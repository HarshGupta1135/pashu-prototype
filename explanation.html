Discovery is the symptom. The real gap is that no animal in India has a health record — every visit starts from zero, from a handwritten slip and the owner’s memory. So: launch as discovery, build as a health record.

01

Product concept

Who uses it, in priority order

UserWhy they come first
Pet parents
metro, 25–40		High frequency, willingness to pay, and the only user whose repeat visits create a record worth keeping.
Rescuers & feedersBursty and urgent. Low revenue, but they generate supply density and the word of mouth that seeds a city.
Vets & clinicsSupply side. Mostly single-doctor practices with no software at all.
NGOs, ambulances, boardingThe long tail with the worst discoverability today.

The problem solved first

“My pet needs care now, I don’t know who is good, open or nearby — and last year’s reports are in a drawer.”

Scope deliberately narrow: one city (Bengaluru), companion animals only, discovery plus records. Not livestock, not multi-city, not marketplace.

Five features, and why these five

  1. Species-aware local search. A rabbit vet is not a dog vet; this shapes the result set rather than sitting in a filter drawer.
  2. Verified provider profile — today’s hours, species handled, fee, ambulance flag, and a visible freshness score.
  3. Pet health vault. Photograph a prescription or report; get a structured, portable timeline.
  4. Auto-built care calendar. Vaccination, deworming and follow-up dates derived from what was scanned, delivered on WhatsApp.
  5. Emergency mode. One screen: nearest open clinic, ambulance on call, exact location as a Plus Code.

Features 3 and 4 are the retention engine. Features 1 and 2 are how anyone arrives. Feature 5 is how a city hears about us.

03

AI & automation

Four workflows, ordered by how much they differentiate. Own concept marks what I am claiming as mine rather than borrowing from existing products.

A · Prescription & report ingestion

A phone photo of a handwritten Indian vet slip becomes structured data, with confidence shown per field and low-confidence values sent back to the owner to confirm rather than silently guessed.

Processes
Photos, lab PDFs, discharge summaries, vaccination cards
Extracts
Clinic, diagnosis, weight, each drug with dose / route / frequency / duration
Automates
Manual data entry, and the follow-up dates that fall out of it

B · Provider verification agent Own concept

Indian directories die of stale data. An agent messages each provider on WhatsApp on a rolling cycle — still open, still these hours, still taking cats — reads the free-text reply, updates the listing, and decays a visible freshness score when nobody answers.

Processes
Maps listings, NGO PDFs, Instagram bios, forwarded WhatsApp lists, provider replies
Automates
The ops team every directory otherwise has to hire

C · Species-aware record intelligence Own concept

Creatinine of 1.4 mg/dL is normal in a dog and alarming in a person. Lab values are read against species and breed reference ranges, shown as a trend against the previous result, with a drafted set of questions to ask the vet. A second check recomputes mg/kg dosing against the extracted body weight and flags mismatches — an unclear digit is a dosing error, not a typo.

Processes
The pet’s own record history plus species reference ranges
Automates
Reading two PDFs side by side to notice a change

D · Rescue triage & case file Own concept

A one-line description becomes an urgency band — go now / within 24 hours / home care — routed to the right kind of provider. Photo plus location then becomes a WhatsApp message and an Instagram caption, which rescuers currently retype a dozen times per animal.

Processes
Free-text symptoms, photo, geolocation, provider capacity
Guardrail
Never a diagnosis, never a drug or dose; red-flag phrases escalate by rule, not by model
04

Technology

Stack

Next.js PWA on Vercel for the client — installable, no app-store friction, one codebase for both user types. Java Spring Boot for the API: a modular monolith with Spring Web, Spring Security (JWT), Spring Data JPA and Flyway migrations. PostgreSQL + PostGIS for providers and geo-search, Redis for sessions and rate limits, S3 for documents. Document extraction runs off a RabbitMQ queue in a separate Spring worker, so an upload never blocks a request.

APIs & integrations

  • Google Maps Places, Geocoding, Distance Matrix — plus Plus Codes for rescue locations.
  • WhatsApp Business Cloud API — the real interface in India: reminders, document upload, provider verification.
  • Claude API — vision extraction and structured output for the document pipeline.
  • MSG91 — phone OTP authentication.
  • Razorpay — scaffolded, switched on only after booking exists.

How it connects

Uploads return immediately and are processed asynchronously; the confirm-screen is served from the worker’s output. One service, one database, no microservices at this size.

05

30 days: what I build, what I cut

Build — weeks 1 to 4

  • W1 · Schema, auth, ~50 Koramangala providers seeded and verified by hand. Data before software.
  • W2 · Search, filters, provider profile, call button. Shippable directory.
  • W3 · The document pipeline: upload → extract → confirm → timeline. The hard part gets the most time.
  • W4 · WhatsApp reminders, emergency screen, 20 real users recruited from two clinics and one rescue group.
The one metric

Not downloads. Share of users who upload at least one record and return in week two. If the vault does not retain, the whole thesis is wrong — and 30 days is enough to find that out.

Deliberately left out

  • Online booking. No small practice runs a calendar; a book button would promise what the clinic cannot keep. A phone call is the honest primitive.
  • Ratings and reviews. Empty review counts are worse than none. Freshness scores and grouped visit reasons carry the trust instead.
  • Payments and commission — nothing to take a cut of yet.
  • Vet-side dashboard, teleconsults, native apps, insurance, e-pharmacy, livestock, second city, any in-house model training.

Each cut buys days for the extraction pipeline, which is the only component a competitor cannot copy in a weekend.
